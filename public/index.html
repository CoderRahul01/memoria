How it works

  1. 1

    Sit with them and press record

    Pick a question if you need one: “What was your first job?” Then just listen. Talk as long as you like, in any language.

  2. 2

    Memoria keeps the words

    Their voice becomes text, gets a title and a year, and goes into their album. The recording itself is deleted.

  3. 3

    Ask about them, any time

    “How did Nani make the dal?” You’ll get the answer with the exact words she said, and which story they came from.
