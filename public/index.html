Clarity & Architecture

How Memoria works

Built for families with open-source AI, zero corporate data harvesting, and complete privacy.

1. Concept

What is Memoria?

Memoria is an open-source, private oral history archive. It enables families to record spoken stories, grandmother recipes, and generational advice, turning speech into searchable wisdom read aloud in their actual voices.

2. Intelligence

Which AI models power the memories?

Memoria uses Google Gemma 4 (31B) open-weight models for memory structuring and life tributes, Backboard.io for memory retrieval (RAG), ElevenLabs for speech-to-text (Scribe) and voice cloning, and Tinker for open-model backup inference.

3. Ethics

How does voice cloning work with family consent?

Memoria enforces strict consent: voice cloning is only activated after affirmative confirmation. Voice audio is processed with ElevenLabs and assigned exclusively to the private family archive. Raw audio is never used to train public foundation models.

4. Sovereignty

Can our family export or print everything?

Yes. Your family has 100% data sovereignty. All memories and metadata are stored in a local SQLite database, downloadable anytime as a standalone JSON file or printable as a keepsake book.
